Industry Context & Market Need

The economic and structural realities of the Indian construction sector
Slide 02 / 15
🔨 Unorganized Workforce
Over 85% of daily-wage tradespersons (plumbers, masons, electricians) operate through informal physical labor stands (nakas), resulting in erratic wages and severe underemployment.
💸 Middlemen Exploitation
Unlicensed intermediaries capture 15%–30% in arbitrary brokerage commissions from workers while providing zero accountability, safety assurances, or price transparency to clients.
📋 Lack of Digitization
Over 90% of small and mid-sized construction contracts rely on verbal agreements and paper registers, leading to chronic payment withholding, attendance fraud, and legal disputes.
🎯 The BuildConnect Transformation
BuildConnect provides a verified, transparent, and direct digital marketplace that combines radius-based emergency job matching, structured multi-bid tenders, and tamper-proof GPS QR attendance.

Problem Statement

Four critical structural challenges addressed by the platform
Slide 03 / 15
1. Discovery & Verification Bottleneck
Property owners have no reliable way to verify the professional identity, police credentials, or past customer ratings of workers entering their residential homes.
2. Contract & Payment Friction
Verbal arrangements lead to scope creep and unpaid balances. Contractors lack formal milestone schedules, and clients have no stage-gate approval mechanism before funds are disbursed.
3. Ghost Worker Attendance Fraud
Contractors on large construction sites suffer from inflated paper muster rolls and proxy check-ins, inflating payroll expenditures by up to 18%.
4. Information Asymmetry in Bidding
Commercial renovation tenders are settled via opaque telephone negotiations without standard line-item deliverables, leading to cost overruns and delays.
🎙️ Speaking Notes (Slide 3):
Our problem statement isolates four distinct pain points: verification deficit, oral contract disputes, ghost worker payroll fraud on construction sites, and opaque bidding negotiations. BuildConnect delivers targeted software solutions for each of these four vectors.

Project Limitations

Transparent technical boundaries of the current implementation
Slide 13 / 15
1. External Payment Webhook Escrow
Financial records are accurately logged in the payments table with transaction references, but automated bank escrow releases (e.g., Razorpay/Stripe webhooks) require commercial merchant KYC credentials.
2. Browser Camera vs Native Hardware
The on-site QR scanner uses the HTML5 navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia API, which depends on browser security policies and HTTPS rather than low-level native mobile camera autofocus.
3. Short-Polling vs WebSockets
System notifications and live status updates currently operate via HTTP short-polling and page navigation events rather than persistent full-duplex WebSocket connections.
4. Offline Tile Caching
Map rendering relies on active internet access for Leaflet and OpenStreetMap tiles; offline map caching for remote, connectivity-deprived construction sites is not yet implemented.
🎙️ Speaking Notes (Slide 13):
In the spirit of rigorous academic evaluation, we have documented four current limitations: payment escrow relies on internal transaction logging pending live banking credentials; QR scanning uses web browser APIs; notifications use HTTP polling; and map rendering requires an active internet connection.
